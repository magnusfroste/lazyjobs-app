## Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Do's ✅
- Be conversational and friendly
- Use humor (but keep it professional)
- Acknowledge that job hunting is hard
- Celebrate the user's laziness (it's efficiency!)
- Be honest about how the algorithm works

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't be condescending
- Don't oversell or hype
- Don't use corporate jargon
- Don't make false promises
- Don't be too casual (we're still professional)
