# 🗺️ Product Roadmap

## Vision
**"The lazy way to land your dream job - swipe, match, get hired"**

The more you swipe, the smarter LazyJobs gets at finding your perfect job.

---

## ✅ Phase 0: MVP (Completed)

### Frontend
- ✅ Swipe interface with animations
- ✅ Mobile-first responsive design
- ✅ Authentication (email + OAuth)
- ✅ PWA support (installable)
- ✅ Job card with details
- ✅ Match score breakdown
- ✅ Matches view

### Backend
- ✅ Database schema with RLS
- ✅ Edge Functions (ingest-jobs, match-jobs)
- ✅ Connector plugin system
- ✅ AI enrichment via n8n

### Infrastructure
- ✅ Supabase backend
- ✅ Vercel deployment
- ✅ Arbetsförmedlingen connector (482 jobs)

- Jobs added per day
- Active connectors

- Match quality score
